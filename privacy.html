Privacy policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

Simple Accountz helps small businesses and individuals keep invoices, expenses, and receipts. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

Using the app without an account

Your books (invoices, clients, expenses, receipt photos, and files) are saved only on your own device. They are not sent to us.

Information we keep when you create an account

  • Your email address, to sign you in, confirm your address, and send password reset links.
  • Your password, stored only as a one-way argon2id hash. We cannot see or recover it.
  • Your books, so they can be backed up and synced between your devices. This includes what you enter: client names and email addresses, invoice lines and amounts, expenses, receipt photos, and files you add.
  • 2-step sign-in, if you turn it on: the secret for your authenticator app and one-way hashes of your backup codes.
  • Sign-in sessions, stored as one-way hashes, which expire after 30 days.

How we use it

Only to run the service: to sign you in, keep your account secure, sync your books, and send the emails you ask for (confirmation and password reset). We do not send marketing email.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you tap it, to photograph a receipt or a paper invoice. Photos stay on your device unless you are signed in, in which case they sync with your books.

Service providers

  • Railway (United States) hosts our server and database.
  • Resend sends account emails on our behalf.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website.
  • Have I Been Pwned: when you choose a password, only the first 5 characters of its SHA-1 hash are checked against known breaches. Your password is never sent.
  • Google Fonts provides the typeface on the website.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with argon2id, attempts are rate limited, and you can turn on 2-step sign-in. In browsers, your session is held in a secure HttpOnly cookie that page scripts cannot read.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account until you delete it. You can delete your account and its cloud copy at any time in the app (Settings, then Account and sync, then Delete account). Deletion is immediate and permanent. See Delete your account. Books saved on a device stay there until you erase them (Settings, then Erase data on this device).

Children

Simple Accountz is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

You can see and edit your information in the app, export it from Reports, and delete it at any time. For any other request, email support@simpleaccountz.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
