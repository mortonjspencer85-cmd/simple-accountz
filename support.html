Common questions

I forgot my passwordOn the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?. We will email a reset link, or you can reset with your authenticator app or a backup code.
My books are not on my other deviceSign in with the same email on both devices. Your books sync automatically. Tap Sync now in Account and sync to sync straight away.
I lost the phone with my authenticator appSign in with one of your backup codes, then turn 2-step sign-in off and on again to scan a new QR code.
How do I give my records to my accountant?Go to Money, then Reports, then Export. You get a spreadsheet of payments, expenses, invoices, and tax collected.
How do I delete my account?See Delete your account.
