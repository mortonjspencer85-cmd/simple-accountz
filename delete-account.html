Delete your account

Last updated November 26, 2026

You can delete your Simple Accountz account, and everything stored with it, at any time.

In the app (fastest)Open Simple Accountz, go to Settings, then Account and sync, then Delete account. Enter your password (and a code from your authenticator app if 2-step sign-in is on). Deletion happens immediately.
By emailIf you cannot sign in, email support@simpleaccountz.com from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm it is your account and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • Your password hash, 2-step sign-in secret, and backup codes
  • Every synced invoice, client, expense, receipt photo, and file
  • All sign-in sessions and email links

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our servers after deletion. Books saved on your own devices stay there until you remove them (Settings, then Erase data on this device). Records of emails sent may remain at our email provider for up to 30 days.
